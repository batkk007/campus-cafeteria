How to Place an Order

  1. Choose Items: Browse our menu categories and select your favorite meals.
  2. Pay at the Counter: Place your order and make the payment at the cash counter.
  3. Collect Token: Get your order collection token and keep it safe.
  4. Pick Up: Collect your freshly prepared food when your token number is called.

Peak Hours & Guidelines

Peak Hours: 1:00 PM - 2:30 PM (Expect longer waiting times).

Pick-up Rules: Please collect your orders promptly from the designated counter. Outside food is strictly not allowed.
