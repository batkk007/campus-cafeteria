How to Place an Order

Follow these simple steps to enjoy your meal at Campus Bites without any hassle.

  1. Choose Items: Browse our menu categories and select your favorite meals.
  2. Pay at the Counter: Place your order and make the payment securely at the cash counter.
  3. Collect Token: Get your unique order collection token and keep it safe.
  4. Pick Up: Collect your freshly prepared food promptly when your token number is called at the counter.

Peak Hours & Important Guidelines

  • Peak Hours: 1:00 PM - 2:30 PM (Expect longer waiting times during these hours).
  • Pick-up Rules: Orders must be collected within 10 minutes of announcement.
  • Policy Notice: Outside food and beverages are strictly not allowed inside the cafeteria premises.
